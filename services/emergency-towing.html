When to call for an emergency tow

Breakdowns never happen at a convenient time. We take calls around the clock for:

  • Engines that stall, overheat or won't start
  • Transmission, brake or steering problems that make the car unsafe to drive
  • Warning lights and strange noises you don't want to risk driving on
  • Vehicles stuck in accessible local areas, in a ditch or in a snowbank
  • Cars that need to get to a mechanic first thing in the morning

What's included

Every emergency tow includes a phone quote before we dispatch, an arrival estimate, proper securing of your vehicle, and delivery to the address you choose — your home, your mechanic, a dealership or a body shop. We tow cars, SUVs, minivans and light-duty trucks.

Local roadside towing

We assist with breakdowns on authorized local streets and accessible private property. Restricted towing zones are excluded. In a restricted zone, call Ontario 511 if safe, or 911 in an emergency.

Where we'll take your car

You decide. In Ontario you have the right to choose where your vehicle is towed (unless police direct otherwise). If you don't have a mechanic, we can suggest some nearby options — the choice is always yours.

Frequently asked questions

Can you tow my car at night or on a holiday?

Yes — we operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.

Can I ride in the tow truck?

Ask when you call. When there's room and it's safe, we can usually take one passenger with the vehicle.

My car is in a parking garage. Can you still tow it?

Often yes. Tell us the location and, if you know it, the garage clearance height, and we'll confirm on the phone.
