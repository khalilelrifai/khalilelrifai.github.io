After an accident: what to do

  1. Make sure everyone is safe. If anyone is injured, call 911 first.
  2. Move to safety if you can. Turn on your hazard lights.
  3. Exchange information with the other driver and take photos of the scene and damage.
  4. Call us at (647) 892-5167 — we'll tell you the price and when we'll arrive.

Your rights in Ontario: you choose who tows your vehicle and where it goes, unless police direct otherwise. You should get the price before the tow and an itemized invoice after. If someone at the scene pressures you to sign, take your time.

Where we can take your vehicle

  • A Collision Reporting Centre — for minor collisions with no injuries, Peel Regional Police may ask you to report there
  • The body shop you or your insurer choose
  • Your dealership or home

Careful recovery of damaged vehicles

Damaged vehicles need extra care — locked wheels, leaking fluids, broken glass and loose parts. We secure everything properly and clean up debris from the vehicle before we leave.

Help with your insurance claim

We'll give you a clear receipt for the tow and can take photos of the vehicle at pickup, which can help your insurance claim.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to use the tow truck that shows up at the accident?

No. In Ontario you have the right to choose your tow company and where your vehicle goes, unless police direct otherwise. You can call us directly.

Can you tow to a Collision Reporting Centre and then to the body shop?

Yes — tell us when you call and we'll plan the route and quote it up front.

Will my insurance cover the tow?

Many policies do. Keep your receipt and check with your insurer; we'll give you everything you need for the claim.
