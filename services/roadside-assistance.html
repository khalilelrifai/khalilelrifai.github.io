Roadside Assistance in Mississauga

Dead battery, flat tire or keys locked in the car? We'll come to you and, in many cases, get you back on the road without a tow.

Battery boost (jump start)

Cold Ontario mornings are hard on batteries. If your car clicks but won't crank, we'll come to your driveway, office lot or roadside location and give you a boost. If the battery won't hold a charge, we can tow you to a shop for a replacement.

Flat tire change

Blown or flat tire? We'll safely put on your spare so you can drive to a tire shop. No usable spare, or a damaged wheel? We'll tow you to the tire shop or mechanic of your choice.

Car lockout service

Keys locked inside, or locked in the trunk? We'll help you get back in. For your protection we'll ask you to show proof that the vehicle is yours.

Where we provide roadside help

Anywhere in Mississauga — home driveways, condo and office parking, plazas, and highway shoulders — plus Brampton, Oakville, Etobicoke, Milton, Toronto.

Frequently asked questions

Is roadside assistance cheaper than a tow?

Usually yes. If a boost, tire change or lockout solves the problem, you avoid a tow entirely. Call and we'll quote both options.

What if the boost doesn't work?

We can tow your vehicle straight to a mechanic or battery shop on the same call.

Do you need anything from me for a lockout?

Yes — we'll ask for ID and proof of ownership (for example the registration, once the car is open) before we hand the vehicle over.
