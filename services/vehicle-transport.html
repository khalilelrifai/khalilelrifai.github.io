Vehicle Transport & Long-Distance Towing

Bought a car out of town? Moving a project car or a vehicle that doesn't run? We move vehicles safely across the GTA and Ontario.

Call (647) 892-5167Text us

What we transport

  • Cars bought from a dealer, auction or private seller
  • Non-running and project vehicles
  • Vehicles going to or from a mechanic, body shop or storage
  • Cars for people moving to or from Mississauga

Local and long-distance

We handle short moves inside Mississauga and Peel, trips across the GTA — Brampton, Oakville, Etobicoke, Milton, Toronto — and longer runs across Ontario. Tell us the pickup and drop-off addresses and we'll quote the trip before we book.

How booking works

  1. Call, text or send the request form with the pickup, drop-off and vehicle details.
  2. We confirm the price and a pickup time that works for you.
  3. We load and secure the vehicle and deliver it to the destination.

Frequently asked questions

Can you pick up a car I bought from a private seller?

Yes. We'll coordinate the pickup time with you and the seller.

Does the car need to run?

No. We transport non-running vehicles too — just tell us when you book so we bring the right equipment.

How far will you go?

Across the GTA and throughout Ontario. Call with the route and we'll give you a quote.
