Privacy note

How we handle the information you send us.

When you send our request form, we receive your name, phone number, optional email, locations and the details you type. We use this only to respond to your request, arrange your tow or service, and keep a record of the job.

Form messages are delivered to our email through the FormSubmit service. We don't sell or share your information for marketing.

This website does not use advertising cookies. Embedded Google Maps may set their own cookies under Google's privacy policy.

To ask about or delete your information, email Abrprotowing@gmail.com.
