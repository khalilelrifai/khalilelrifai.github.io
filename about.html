About ABR Pro Towing

A towing and roadside assistance company serving Mississauga and the GTA, built on answering the phone and treating every car like our own.

Who we are

ABR Pro Towing provides towing and roadside assistance in Mississauga and across the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), Ontario. We help drivers who are stuck — broken down on a local street, locked out at the mall, dealing with a dead battery on a cold morning, or shaken up after a collision — and we move vehicles for people and businesses across the GTA.

We keep it simple: answer the phone 24/7, give a clear price before we dispatch, provide an arrival estimate, and handle every vehicle carefully.

What we stand for

  • Honest pricing. You get the price before we send a truck — no surprises on the invoice.
  • Respect for your choice. Your vehicle goes where you want it to go.
  • Safety. For you on the roadside, and for your vehicle on our truck.
  • Local service. We serve drivers in Mississauga and across the GTA, subject to location and dispatch availability.
