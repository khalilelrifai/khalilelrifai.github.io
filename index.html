FAQ

Towing questions, answered

How fast can a tow truck get to me in Mississauga?

We dispatch as soon as you call and give you an honest arrival time on the phone based on where you are, traffic and the time of day. Restricted towing zones are excluded. Call 911 if there is immediate danger.

How much does a tow cost in Mississauga?

The price depends on the distance, your vehicle and the situation (for example a simple breakdown versus an accident or a car stuck in a ditch). Call or text us with your location and destination and we'll quote you before we send a truck — no surprises.

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. ABR Pro Towing & Recovery answers calls 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Can you tow my car to my own mechanic or body shop?

Yes. In Ontario you have the right to choose where your vehicle is towed (unless police direct otherwise). We'll take it to your home, your mechanic, your dealership or the body shop your insurer recommends.

Do you help if I just need a boost, a tire change or a lockout?

Yes. Our roadside assistance covers battery boosts, flat tire changes using your spare, and car lockouts — often no tow is needed.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Mississauga and cover Mississauga neighbourhoods plus Brampton, Oakville, Etobicoke, Milton and Toronto. See our service areas.
